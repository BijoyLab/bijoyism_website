### English public company description

BIJOYISM is a children's sensory-toy brand operated by Shenzhen Hengzhian Technology Co., Ltd., registered at Room 301, No. 644, Longxiang Avenue, Longcheng Street, Longgang District, Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China, and established in January 2019. The brand designs and sells sensory toys for children aged 3 and up, with liquid sensory floor tiles as its core category. BIJOYISM primarily serves customers in the United States and China and can serve customers worldwide.

## 品牌定位底稿

### 中文

BIJOYISM 专注为 3 岁及以上儿童设计感官玩具，核心产品是液体感官地垫。孩子用手掌按压或用脚踩踏时，地垫内的彩色液体随之流动，配合印刷画面形成可看、可踩、可讲故事的多感官体验。产品面向家庭游戏区、学前活动角与专业感官训练室三类场景，主要服务美国和中国市场，并可服务全球市场。

### English

BIJOYISM designs sensory toys for children aged 3 and up. Its core product, the liquid sensory floor tile, shifts colored liquid under hand or foot pressure and pairs movement with printed artwork for multisensory play in family play areas, preschool activity corners, and professional sensory training rooms. The brand primarily serves the United States and China and can serve customers worldwide.
